# IMP-097 - "Remind me" and "Something old"

<objective>
Saved things come back at the right time: a person can ask to be reminded
of an item on a date, and can ask Memoria to show something they saved
long ago.
</objective>

<context>
Approved by the owner on 2026-11-11. Users of rival link savers have asked
for this for two years; there is no proof yet that it brings people back,
so keep it small. Memoria has no reminders. It has notifications
(`NotificationType`, `NotificationPreference`), a scheduler
(`scripts/scheduler.mjs`, `/api/cron/maintenance`), the outbox for
background work, a record of when an item was last opened
(`/api/v1/items/[itemId]/opened`), and an AI "serendipity" dialog that
needs an AI key.
</context>

<files>
`prisma/schema.prisma`, `scripts/scheduler.mjs`,
`src/app/api/cron/maintenance/route.ts`, `src/lib/outbox/`,
`src/app/api/v1/notifications/`, `src/features/canvas/components/`,
`src/features/dashboard/components/DashboardContent.tsx`,
`tests/integration/`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
Schema change with a Prisma migration. Sending a reminder is an outbox job
that is safe to run twice. A reminder belongs to the person who set it and
is checked against their access to the item when it fires. "Something old"
uses no AI.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- An item's menu offers "Remind me": tomorrow, next week, or a date.
- On that day the person gets a notification that opens the item, and an
  email only if they chose email notifications.
- A list shows upcoming reminders and lets the person change or cancel
  them. Deleting the item or losing access cancels its reminders.
- The dashboard offers "Something old": one item not opened for at least
  30 days, with "Show another".
</acceptance>

<verification>

</verification>
