# IMP-050 - Upload body and image bounds

<objective>
Upload size is enforced before the body is buffered, on any ingress, and decoded images are bounded.
</objective>

<files>
`src/app/api/v1/upload/route.ts`, `src/proxy.ts`, `Caddyfile`
</files>

<acceptance>
- Requests over the limit get 413 from the app without buffering the body, with or without Caddy.
- Images over a pixel or frame bound are rejected.
</acceptance>

<verification>
Route tests with oversized and decode-bomb fixtures; `pnpm test -- --run`.
</verification>
