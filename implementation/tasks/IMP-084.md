# IMP-084 - Canvas: clearer top bar and add bar

<objective>
A newcomer can tell what each canvas control does. Today the top bar is one
long row of unlabelled icons and a switch that reads "MANUAL / ORGANIZER".
</objective>

<context>
From the 2026-11-11 UI review. `CanvasHeader.tsx` (about 690 lines) holds
the title, the view switch (`ToggleButton` "Manual" / "Organizer") and most
actions; `CanvasSecondaryActions.tsx` and `MainToolbar.tsx` hold the rest.
The Organizer is the accessible structured view and the launch contract
test expects exactly one of it on editable and public routes.
</context>

<files>
`src/features/canvas/components/CanvasHeader.tsx`,
`src/features/canvas/components/CanvasSecondaryActions.tsx`,
`src/features/canvas/components/MainToolbar.tsx`,
`tests/unit/launch-surface-contract.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
Nothing is removed and keyboard shortcuts stay the same. Viewers see only
what they may use; the HTTP routes remain the real permission check. Keep
the phone layout from IMP-078.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- Three groups: an "add" bar at the bottom centre with labelled buttons for
  the things you can add; view tools (zoom, fit, grid) in one corner; people
  present, Share and "More" at the top right.
- Less-used actions (export, history, timer and similar) sit under "More"
  with text labels.
- The view switch uses plain sentence-case names with a tooltip that says
  what each view does (proposed: "Free layout" and "Organised list"); the
  saved choice and behaviour are unchanged.
- Every icon-only button has a tooltip and an accessible name.
</acceptance>
