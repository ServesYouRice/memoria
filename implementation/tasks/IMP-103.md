# IMP-103 - The canvas stays smooth when it is full

<objective>
A canvas at its limit of 2,000 items still moves, zooms and drags
smoothly, and a repeatable check proves it.
</objective>

<context>
Approved by the owner on 2026-11-11: slow big boards are the top complaint
against the market leader, and Memoria draws its canvas with its own
Konva-based engine. The server side already has budgets and regression
sizes of 500, 1,000 and 2,000 items (`docs/operations/resource-budgets.md`,
viewport pages of 250 detailed items). Nothing measures how the canvas
feels in the browser at that size.
</context>

<files>
`docs/operations/resource-budgets.md`, `src/features/canvas/components/`,
`tests/e2e/`, `scripts/gate.mjs`, `prisma/seed.ts`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
The speed check is an explicit opt-in gate stage, not part of the fast
gate. Keep the 2,000-item limit. Fix what the measurement shows; do not
rewrite the engine.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- A seeded canvas with 2,000 mixed items can be opened in a browser test
  that measures opening time and frame times while panning, zooming and
  dragging a note.
- The measured numbers and the agreed budgets are written into the
  resource budgets document.
- The check fails when a budget is missed, and the worst causes found in
  the first run are fixed.
</acceptance>

<verification>
The new gate stage on the dev machine, three runs with their numbers
reported; `pnpm gate fast`.
</verification>
