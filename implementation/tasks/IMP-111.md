A visitor learns in plain words the things Memoria does that rivals do
not, and a self-hoster learns what is promised.
</objective>

is ahead on: a large free allowance (200 canvases, 2,000 items each), AI

free passkeys and two-step codes, and getting everything out (PNG, PDF,
