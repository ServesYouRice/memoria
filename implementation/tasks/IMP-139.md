# IMP-139 - On a phone, hidden tools get a "More" button and labels fit

<objective>
On a phone every canvas tool can be reached without guessing: the
toolbar shows five tools and a "More" button for the rest. The Ask
button no longer shows a cut-off label, the Settings tabs hint that
more exist, and tap targets are at least 44px.
</objective>

<context>
Found in the UI audit of 2026-11-14, at 390px wide (also checked at
320px):
- The canvas toolbar (`CanvasAddBar`) holds Select, Pan, Note, Link,
  Image, Text, Shape, Arrow, Draw, and Poll and Table where enabled. On
  a phone it is cut after "Arrow". The bar scrolls sideways
  (`overflowX: "auto"`) but nothing hints at that, so Draw, Poll and
  Table are out of sight.
- The top bar's Ask button shows "Ask your canvases…" squeezed down to
  "Ask y…".
- The Settings section tabs scroll sideways with
  `scrollButtons={false}`, so sections past the screen edge are easy to
  miss.
- Measured in a phone-sized window with touch turned on: top-bar icon
  buttons 40x40, avatar button about 32x32, Ask button 32px high,
  footer links 39px high, ordinary buttons 41-43px high. The app's own
  rule is 44px (`TOUCH_TARGET` in `src/lib/theme.ts`, applied under
  `@media (pointer: coarse)`). These numbers come from an emulated
  phone, not a real one: measure again in step 5 before changing sizes.
Rule used (NN/g): if more exists, show that it does.
</context>

<files>
`src/features/canvas/components/CanvasAddBar.tsx`,
`src/features/command-palette/AskPalette.tsx` (`AskLauncher`),
`src/app/settings/SettingsContent.tsx` (the `Tabs`),
`src/components/layout/AppShell.tsx` (top bar, footer),
`src/lib/theme.ts` (`touchTarget`),
`tests/e2e/canvas-mobile-toolbar.spec.ts` (pattern for measuring).
</files>

<executor_constraints>
- Computer layout (from the `sm` breakpoint up) of the canvas toolbar
  stays exactly as it is.
- Do not remove a tool. Poll and Table keep their existing "only when
  enabled" checks.
- Mouse users keep today's compact sizes: size changes go inside
  `@media (pointer: coarse)` only.
- Do not change what the Ask button does.
</executor_constraints>

<steps>
1. `CanvasAddBar.tsx`, below the `sm` breakpoint only: show `Select`,
   `Pan`, `Note`, `Link`, `Image` and then a button `More` (icon
   `MoreHoriz`, `aria-label="More tools"`, `aria-haspopup="menu"`).
   `More` opens a MUI `Menu` above the bar that lists the remaining
   entries with icon and name: `Text`, `Shape`, `Arrow`, `Draw`, and
   `Poll` and `Table` when they exist. Picking one does exactly what
   its bar button does and closes the menu.
2. When the active tool is one of the tools inside `More`, draw the
   `More` button as pressed (`aria-pressed="true"`) and show that
   tool's icon on it, so the person can see which tool is on.
3. Remove sideways scrolling from the bar on phones: with six buttons
   it must fit at 320px wide without scrolling.
4. `AskLauncher` (`AskPalette.tsx`): below the `md` breakpoint, replace
   the pill with the text "Ask your canvases…" by the same icon button
   the computer layout uses (`aria-label="Ask Memoria"`). Remove the
   `flexGrow` that made the pill stretch. Check the top bar still
   lines up at 390px and 320px.
5. Tap sizes. Using a Playwright context with `hasTouch: true` and
   `isMobile: true` at 390x844, measure every button and link in the
   top bar and footer of `/dashboard` and `/settings`, and the canvas
   toolbar. For each control smaller than 44x44, make it at least 44x44
   under `@media (pointer: coarse)`: prefer extending `touchTarget` in
   `src/lib/theme.ts` to the component type (icon buttons, small
   buttons) over one-off styles. The avatar picture may stay 32px; its
   button must be 44px.
6. `SettingsContent.tsx`: change `scrollButtons={false}` to
   `scrollButtons="auto"` and add `allowScrollButtonsMobile`, so arrows
   appear when sections are off screen.
7. Tests: add `tests/e2e/phone-reachability.spec.ts` (follow
   `canvas-mobile-toolbar.spec.ts`), at 390x844 and 320x640 with touch:
   - the canvas toolbar shows `More tools`; its menu lists `Draw`;
     choosing `Draw` marks `More tools` as pressed;
   - the toolbar's scroll width equals its visible width;
   - every button in the top bar is at least 44x44;
   - the page does not scroll sideways.
   Add a unit test for the `More` menu contents in
   `tests/unit/canvas-add-bar-more.test.tsx`.
</steps>

<acceptance>
- At 390px and 320px wide the canvas toolbar shows six buttons, none
  cut off, and all other tools are one tap away under `More`.
- From the `sm` breakpoint up the toolbar looks as it did before.
- No "Ask y…" text: the phone top bar shows an Ask icon button.
- Settings on a phone shows scroll arrows when sections do not fit.
- On a touch screen every top-bar control measures at least 44x44.
</acceptance>
