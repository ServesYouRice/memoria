# IMP-119 - Settings page shows one copy of each field while it loads

<objective>
`tests/e2e/sign-in-security.spec.ts` passes again: the Security settings
page never holds two copies of its fields at once.
</objective>

<context>
Found on 2026-11-14 while running the Chromium browser suite for IMP-117.
Right after `/settings/security` opens, the page briefly holds two full
copies of the settings group (the number of `current-password` fields goes
2, 4, then back to 2 within about 200 ms). The test's
`hydrated(getByLabel("Password to confirm"))` lands in that moment and
fails in strict mode, also on retry. After the page settles there is one
copy. One copy carries a server-rendered id (`_R_…`) and the other a
client id (`_r_…`), which points at a hydration or remount problem rather
than at the test. No hydration error is logged in production mode.
</context>

<files>
`src/app/settings/[[...section]]/page.tsx`, `src/app/settings/SettingsContent.tsx`,
`src/components/layout/AppShell.tsx`, `tests/e2e/sign-in-security.spec.ts`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
Find why a second copy mounts and fix that; do not relax the test's strict
locator. Check whether other pages do the same.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- The Security settings page holds one copy of its fields from first paint.
- The two-factor browser test passes in Chromium without a retry.
</acceptance>

<verification>
`node scripts/run-e2e.mjs --project=chromium tests/e2e/sign-in-security.spec.ts`;
`pnpm gate e2e` on the dev machine.
</verification>
