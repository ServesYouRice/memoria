# IMP-079 - Brand colours and font from the logo

<objective>
The app looks like Memoria, not like a generic purple app: its colours come
from the logo and it has its own typeface.
</objective>

<context>
The 2026-11-11 UI review found an indigo/violet gradient look (`brand`,
`gradients`, `glassStyles` in `src/lib/theme.ts`) and the plain system font,
while the logo already has its own colours: navy `#1c2452`, light blue
`#93c5fd`, yellow `#fbbf24`, red `#e11d48`. Approved direction: navy for
headings and dark surfaces; one solid blue (about `#2c5fc9`) for main
buttons, links and focus rings; yellow as the highlight; red only for
errors, destructive actions and unread counts; no gradients on buttons, the
top bar or avatars. Fonts in the approved sketch: Bricolage Grotesque for
headings and Figtree for text.
</context>

<files>
`src/lib/theme.ts`, `src/app/layout.tsx`, `src/components/MemoriaLogo.tsx`,
`tests/unit/launch-surface-contract.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/visual/`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
- Fonts ship with the app (for example `@fontsource-variable/*` or
  `next/font/local`): the build needs no network access to a font host, the
  browser makes no third-party font request, and the CSP is unchanged.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and icons) in light
  and dark. Keep the launch contract test's contrast rule meaningful.
- Change the theme only; do not redesign screens here. Fix only what the new
  theme visibly breaks.
- Keep exported theme names that other files import, or update every
  importer in the same change.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- Main buttons, links, focus rings and selected states use the solid brand
  blue; no gradient remains on buttons, the top bar or avatars.
- Headings use the heading font and text uses the text font, both served
  from the app's own address.
- Yellow appears only as a highlight; red only for errors, destructive
  actions and unread counts.
- The logo reads well on the light and the dark top bar.
- Light and dark pass the axe checks in `tests/e2e/launch-surface.spec.ts`.
</acceptance>

`pnpm test:file tests/unit/launch-surface-contract.test.ts`;

the visual baselines refreshed; screenshots of home, sign-in, dashboard,
canvas and settings at 1440x900 and 390x844, light and dark.
</verification>
