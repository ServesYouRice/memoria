# IMP-115 - A hosted paid plan people can sign up and pay for

<objective>
Someone who does not want to run a server can use Memoria hosted by the
owner, start free, and pay for more.
</objective>

<context>
The owner chose on 2026-11-11 to plan a hosted paid version. Nothing for
it exists: there is no billing code, no plans and no payment company.
`src/lib/policy/launch-limits.ts` holds one set of limits for everyone,
and `tests/unit/launch-surface-contract.test.ts` keeps the home page from
promising more than is true. DEC-017 promises that self-hosting, passkeys
and two-step codes, export, and AI on your own key stay free. DEC-019
holds what the paid plan adds, its price and the payment company.
</context>

<files>
`prisma/schema.prisma`, `src/lib/policy/launch-limits.ts`,
`src/lib/policy/capacity.ts`, `src/app/api/v1/webhooks/`,
`src/app/settings/`, `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/terms/`,
`src/app/privacy/`, `docs/operations/`,
`tests/unit/launch-surface-contract.test.ts`, `tests/integration/`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
- Billing is off by default. A self-hosted Memoria shows no prices, no
  plans and no upgrade prompts, and loses nothing.
- Nothing promised free in DEC-017 is ever limited by plan.
- Card details never touch Memoria: the payment company's own page takes
  them. Its messages to Memoria are checked for a valid signature and are
  safe to receive twice. Keys go in the environment, never in the code.
- The plan a person is on is decided on the server; the page only shows
  it. Limits are enforced where they are today.
- Falling behind on payment never deletes anything: the account drops to
  the free limits, stays readable and can still export.
- Schema change with a Prisma migration. This is a large card: consult a
  higher-tier model on the design before building.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- With billing on, a person can see the plans, pay, see their plan and
  next payment date in Settings, change or cancel it, and get receipts.
- Paying raises the person's limits at once; cancelling lowers them at
  the end of the paid period.
- A failed or repeated payment message leaves the account correct.
- The home page, terms and privacy pages state the plans and limits
  truthfully, and the launch-surface test still passes.
- With billing off, no plan or price appears anywhere.
</acceptance>

<verification>
`pnpm test:file` for the billing, limit and webhook tests; `pnpm gate
fast`; `pnpm gate integration` and `pnpm gate e2e` on the dev machine; one
real payment and cancellation in the payment company's test mode.
</verification>
