# IMP-131 - AI settings fit one screen, in plain words

<objective>
Settings -> AI assistant shows three short cards (connect, what AI may
do, used today). Everything about agents, extra providers and history
sits in one "Advanced" block that is closed by default, and its wording
is in everyday language.
</objective>

<context>
Found in the UI audit of 2026-11-14. The page is about 3,200px tall
(three and a half screens). A simple three-step setup is followed by
text such as "0 of 100,000 worst-case tokens reserved today across 0
requests", "Cost allowance: $0.0000 of $0.1000. Concurrent requests:
0/2. Rejected today: 0.", "Agent control center", "configured BYOK
providers" and "Reverts are change-set driven". Most people only need
the first card.
Rule used: show the everyday options; tuck the rest behind "Advanced".
Other screens link to `/settings/ai#agent-console` (see
`LEGACY_SETTINGS_ANCHORS` in `src/app/settings/sections.ts` and the
button in `CanvasOrganizerView.tsx`). That link must keep working.
</context>

<files>
`src/app/settings/SettingsContent.tsx` (`AiGroup`, `AiUsageSection`),
`src/app/settings/AiProviderSettings.tsx`,
`src/app/settings/LinkEnrichmentSettings.tsx`,
`src/app/settings/AutoFileSettings.tsx`,
`src/features/agents/components/AgentControlCenter.tsx`,
`src/app/settings/sections.ts`, tests that mention these texts
(`grep -rnE "AI usage|Agent control center|worst-case" tests`).
</files>

<executor_constraints>
- Do not change any API call, limit, or what the switches do.
- Do not remove any information: numbers that leave the main view move
  into "Advanced".
- Keep an element with `id="agent-console"` on the page.
</executor_constraints>

<steps>
Part A - layout (`AiGroup` in `SettingsContent.tsx`)
1. Section 1: title `Connect your AI` (was `AI provider`). It contains
   only `<AiProviderSettings />`.
2. Section 2: new `SettingsSection` titled `What AI may do`. Move
   `<LinkEnrichmentSettings />` and `<AutoFileSettings />` into it.
3. Section 3: title `Used today` (was `AI usage`). Show one sentence and
   the progress bar:
   - when AI is on: `P% of today's AI allowance used (N requests).` where
     P is `tokensUsed / tokenLimit` as a whole percent and N is
     `requests` (write `1 request` for one);
   - when nothing was used: `Nothing used today.`;
   - keep the existing "disabled by the operator" and "temporarily
     unavailable" messages, and the loading text.
   Change the bar's `aria-label` to `Share of today's AI allowance used`.
   Remove the caption line that starts `Cost allowance:` from this card
   (it moves in step 5).
4. Below section 3 add a MUI `Accordion` with `id="agent-console"`,
   closed by default, summary text `Advanced: agents, more providers,
   history`. When the page opens with `#agent-console` in the address,
   the accordion starts open and is scrolled into view.
5. Inside the accordion, in this order:
   - a small "Details" block with the exact numbers that were removed in
     step 3, in plain words:
     `Tokens set aside today: X of Y`, `Cost so far today: $A of $B`,
     `Requests running now: C of D`, `Requests refused today: E`;
   - `<AgentControlCenter />`;
   - the "Developer settings" block, if IMP-127 has not already removed
     it.
6. In `src/app/settings/sections.ts` change the `ai` intro to
   `Connect your own AI key and choose what AI may do for you.`

Part B - plain words in `AgentControlCenter.tsx` (Old -> New). Find each
old text with grep; if the exact old sentence differs slightly, replace
the sentence that starts the same way.
- `Agent control center` -> `AI helpers and history`
- the sentence starting `Review agent profiles, configured BYOK
  providers` -> `See your AI helpers, the AI services you connected,
  suggestions waiting for you, and what AI has changed.`
- `Agent profiles` -> `AI helpers`; `No agent profiles configured yet.`
  -> `No AI helpers set up yet.`; `Agent profiles could not be loaded.`
  -> `AI helpers could not be loaded.`
- `Provider credentials` -> `Connected AI services`; `Provider
  credentials could not be loaded.` -> `Connected AI services could not
  be loaded.`; chip `not configured` -> `not connected`
- `No integration accounts configured yet.` -> `No integrations
  connected yet.`
- `Suggestion queue` -> `Suggestions waiting`
- the sentence starting `Approve or reject low-risk proposals first` ->
  `Approve or reject each suggestion. Bigger changes ask you to confirm
  a second time.`
- `Rollback and history` -> `Undo and history`
- the sentence starting `Reverts are change-set driven` -> `If an AI
  change went wrong, undo the whole change here in one step.`
- `No change sets are waiting to be rolled back.` -> `Nothing to undo.`
- count chips: `N agents` -> `N helpers`; `N/M providers configured` ->
  `N of M services connected`
Part C - tests
7. Update tests that look for the old texts. Add
   `tests/unit/settings-ai-layout.test.tsx`: the Advanced accordion is
   closed by default; it is open when `location.hash` is
   `#agent-console`; the "Used today" sentence shows a percent and never
   the word `worst-case`.
</steps>

- Saving a key, the two switches and all agent actions still work.
