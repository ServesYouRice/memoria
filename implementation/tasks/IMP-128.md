# IMP-128 - Password and email pages look and read like the sign-in page

<objective>
"Forgot password", "Reset password" and "Verify email" use the same
layout, logo and wording as the sign-in page, so they clearly belong to
the same app.
</objective>

<context>
Found in the UI audit of 2026-11-14. The sign-in and create-account pages
use `AuthLayout` (navy panel with the logo and a canvas picture on the
left, form on the right; on a phone only the logo). One click away, the
three pages in this card show a bare white card with no logo, and use
different words: "Forgot Password?", "Back to Login", "Email Address",
"Send Reset Instructions", "Sign Up" (as a default-blue underlined link).
On the verify-email page a failed link shows its whole heading in red.
Rule used (WCAG 3.2.4): the same thing looks and is named the same way
everywhere. Sentence case for all labels.
The sign-in page's own words are the reference: "Sign in", "Create
account", "Forgot password?".
</context>

<files>
`src/app/auth/forgot-password/page.tsx`,
`src/app/auth/reset-password/page.tsx`,
`src/app/auth/verify-email/page.tsx`,
`src/features/auth/components/AuthLayout.tsx` (use as is; it takes
`children` and an optional `headline`),
`src/features/auth/components/LoginForm.tsx` (reference for field, button
and link styling), `tests/e2e/auth-recovery.spec.ts`,
`tests/e2e/auth-layout.spec.ts`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
- Do not change what the forms send or how tokens are checked. Keep every
  `fetch`, validation rule and redirect as it is.
- Do not change `AuthLayout.tsx`, `LoginForm.tsx` or `RegisterForm.tsx`.
- Keep the success message of "forgot password" as it is. It must never
  say whether the email address has an account.
- Links use the same link component and colour as the "Forgot password?"
  link in `LoginForm.tsx`. No browser-default blue underlined links.
</executor_constraints>

<steps>
1. In each of the three pages, replace the outer page wrapper and the
   white `Paper` card with `<AuthLayout headline="...">` around the same
   inner content. Headlines:
   - forgot password: `Back on your canvases in a minute.`
   - reset password: `Choose a new password and carry on.`
   - verify email: `One quick check, then you are in.`
2. Forgot password page, text changes (Old -> New):
   - `Forgot Password?` -> `Reset your password`
   - the intro sentence -> `Enter your email address and we will send you
     a link to choose a new password.`
   - `Email Address` -> `Email address`
   - `Send Reset Instructions` -> `Send reset link`
   - `Back to Login` -> `Back to sign in`
   - `Don't have an account? Sign Up` -> `No account yet? Create account`
     (the words "Create account" are the link to `/auth/register`).
3. Reset password page, text changes:
   - heading `Reset Password` -> `Choose a new password`
   - button `Reset Password` -> `Save new password`;
     busy text `Resetting...` -> `Saving…`
   - `New Password` -> `New password`
   - Remove the `Confirm New Password` field and its "do not match" check.
     Add a show/hide eye to "New password" (copy the pattern from
     `LoginForm.tsx`: `showPassword` state, `Visibility`/`VisibilityOff`,
     `aria-label` "Show password"/"Hide password").
   - any `Back to Login` or `Sign In` link -> `Back to sign in`.
4. Verify email page: the heading shown when a link fails must use the
   normal heading colour. Remove `color="error"` from that heading. The
   reason stays in the red `Alert` below it. Any `Sign In` / `Back to
   Login` text -> `Back to sign in`.
5. Each page keeps exactly one `h1` (the heading inside the form).
6. Update `tests/e2e/auth-recovery.spec.ts` and any unit test that looks
   for the old words (`grep -rnE "Forgot Password|Back to Login|Send Reset Instructions|Reset Password|Confirm New Password" tests`).
</steps>

<acceptance>
- At 1440px wide all three pages show the navy brand panel with the
  Memoria logo on the left, like `/auth/login`. At 390px wide they show
  the logo above the form and do not scroll sideways.
- None of these strings remain in the three pages: `Forgot Password?`,
  `Back to Login`, `Email Address`, `Send Reset Instructions`, `Sign Up`,
  `Reset Password`, `Confirm New Password`.
- Asking for a reset link, choosing a new password with a valid link, and
  verifying an email address all still work.
- No heading on these pages is red.
</acceptance>
