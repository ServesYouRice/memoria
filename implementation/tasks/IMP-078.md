# IMP-078 - Canvas top bar fits on a phone

<objective>
On a phone the canvas top bar is tidy and every action can be reached. Today
its controls overlap each other and some are cut off.
</objective>

<context>
Found in the 2026-11-11 UI review at 390x844. `CanvasHeader.tsx` lays the
title, the Manual/Organizer switch and the action buttons out in one row
that does not shrink; `CanvasSecondaryActions.tsx` holds the extra actions.
</context>

<files>
`src/features/canvas/components/CanvasHeader.tsx`,
`src/features/canvas/components/CanvasSecondaryActions.tsx`,
`tests/e2e/`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
Desktop layout stays as it is (IMP-084 reorganises it). No action is
removed; viewer and editor permissions stay enforced by the HTTP routes.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- At 390x844 and 360x740 nothing in the canvas top bar overlaps or is
  clipped, and the page does not scroll sideways.
- Back, a shortened canvas title, Share and a "More" menu are visible; every
  other action is reachable from "More" with a text label.
- Tap targets in the bar are at least 44x44px.
- A Playwright check at 390px wide fails if two top-bar controls overlap.
</acceptance>

<verification>
`pnpm gate fast`; `pnpm gate e2e` on the dev machine; screenshots of a
canvas at 390x844 in light and dark.
</verification>
