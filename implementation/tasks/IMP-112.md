# IMP-112 - Publish a licence and a "never paywalled" promise

<objective>
Anyone deciding whether to trust Memoria can read under which terms they
may use it and which features will always be free.
</objective>

<context>
Approved by the owner on 2026-11-11 after the competitor review: people
who host their own tools have watched several projects tighten their terms
and now look for this first. The repository has no licence file and no
`license` field in `package.json`. The owner chose (DEC-017, 2026-11-11):
AGPL-3.0, with self-hosting, passkeys and two-step codes, export, and AI
on your own key promised to stay free.
</context>

<files>
`LICENSE`, `package.json`, `README.md`, `src/app/help/page.tsx`,
`src/app/terms/`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
Use the exact, unmodified text of the chosen licence. Before publishing,
list the licences of the shipped dependencies and confirm none conflicts
with the choice; report any that does instead of working around it.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- The repository has the licence file, the `license` field and a short
  README section in plain words.
- Help lists the features promised to stay free, as chosen in DEC-017.
- The dependency licence check and its result are reported.
</acceptance>

<verification>
`pnpm gate fast`; `pnpm gate audit`; the dependency licence list.
</verification>
