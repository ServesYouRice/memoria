# IMP-095 - Arrows work as real links between items

<objective>
Drawing an arrow from one item to another connects them for real: search,
"Ask", AI agents and exports all know the two items are linked.
</objective>

<context>
Approved by the owner on 2026-11-11: this is the most praised idea in the
category. Memoria has two halves that do not meet. Arrows drawn on a canvas
are `ARROW` items whose content can hold `startItemId` and `endItemId`
(`arrowContentSchema` in `src/lib/validation/canvas-item.ts`,
`ArrowItem.tsx`). Separately there is an `ItemConnection` table with
routes under `/api/v1/canvases/[canvasId]/connections` and a hook
(`src/lib/hooks/use-item-connections.ts`); the agent service and Ask
commands read and move those rows (`src/lib/agents/service-core.ts`,
`src/lib/ai/ask-commands.ts`), but no canvas component uses the hook.
</context>

<files>
`src/features/canvas/components/ArrowItem.tsx`,

</files>

<executor_constraints>
Choose one source of truth for "these two items are linked" and keep the
other in step with it in the same transaction; do not leave two records
that can disagree. Existing arrows keep working, and a migration or
backfill covers them. Links never cross canvases, as today.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- An arrow attached to two items creates a link between them; moving,
  deleting or re-attaching the arrow updates or removes the link, also
  through undo and version restore.
- Selecting an item shows what it is linked to and lets the person jump
  there. An arrow can carry a short label.
- Search results and "Ask" answers can use links ("what is connected to
  this?"), and agents read them through the existing tools.
- Markdown and JSON exports include the links.
</acceptance>
