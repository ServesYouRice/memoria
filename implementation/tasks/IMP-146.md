# IMP-146 - A switch that keeps passkeys off until the final domain

<objective>
A setting turns passkeys off; while off, no passkey route or button is
offered.
</objective>

<context>
Handbook ME-08 and owner decision OWN-07: apps stay on the umbrella domain
until their final domain, and passkeys are bound to the domain they were
made on. Today passkeys are always on (`src/lib/auth/server.ts`).
</context>

<files>
`src/lib/auth/server.ts`, `src/lib/env.ts`, sign-in and Security settings UI,
`.env.example`.
</files>

<acceptance>
- Passkeys off: the plugin is not registered, its routes answer 404, the UI
  hides passkey actions.
- Passkeys on: today's behaviour unchanged.
</acceptance>

<verification>
`pnpm gate fast`; `pnpm gate e2e` for the sign-in flow.
</verification>
