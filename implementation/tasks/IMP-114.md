# IMP-114 - Video cards with time-stamped notes and a transcript

<objective>
A saved video is more than a link: a person can keep notes tied to moments
in it, and its transcript can be searched and asked about.
</objective>

<context>
Approved by the owner on 2026-11-11 with the competitor review ("richer
video items"). Today a video link is a static "Link preview" card
(`src/features/canvas/components/EmbedItem.tsx`); nothing from another
site runs inside the canvas (DEC-011, `src/lib/product-surfaces.ts`), and
search only sees the link's address. IMP-094 adds the store for saved
text; this card reuses it. DEC-018 decides whether transcripts may be
fetched automatically, because YouTube offers no open official way to
read the captions of other people's videos.
</context>

<executor_constraints>
- DEC-011 stands: the video opens in a new tab; nothing from another site
  is loaded or run inside the canvas, and the CSP stays as it is.
- Follow the choice recorded in DEC-018. If automatic fetching is allowed
  it is off by default, switched on per deployment, goes through the
  SSRF-safe fetcher, runs as an outbox job that is safe to run twice, and
  fails quietly when a video has no captions.
- A transcript is untrusted text: shown as plain text only and marked as
  data wherever it is given to AI or agents. Set a size limit.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- A video card shows the video's title and where it is from.
- "Add note at a time" makes a note that keeps the video and the moment;
  choosing the time opens the video at that moment in a new tab.
- A person can paste a transcript onto a video card; search and "Ask"
  then find words that appear only in the transcript.
- With automatic fetching allowed and switched on, a video with public
  captions gets its transcript without pasting; with it off, nothing is
  fetched.
- Notes and transcripts are deleted with the item and are part of the
  account export.
</acceptance>
