# IMP-140 - Press and hold opens an item's menu on touch screens

<objective>
On a phone or tablet, pressing and holding a canvas item opens the same
menu that right-click opens on a computer, and the Reminders page tells
phone users to press and hold, not to "right-click".
</objective>

<context>
Found in the UI audit of 2026-11-14.
- Sure: the empty Reminders page says `Right-click an item on a canvas
  and choose "Remind me…" to see it again on a day you pick.` A phone
  has no right-click.
- About 70% sure, NOT confirmed: on an iPhone, press-and-hold on a
  canvas item may not open the item menu at all. The canvas only
  listens for the `contextmenu` event (`onContextMenu` in
  `CanvasBoard.tsx`, `CanvasItemLayer.tsx`, `NoteItem.tsx`); there is
  no touch-hold code. Android Chrome fires `contextmenu` on a long
  press; iOS Safari normally does not. The audit used a phone-sized
  window, not a real phone.
The item menu holds commands such as "Remind me…" that may have no
other door on a phone, so on iPhone they may be unreachable.
</context>

<executor_constraints>
- Step 1 comes first. If a real iPhone or iPad is not available, do
  steps 5 and 6 only (wording), move this card to WAITING and add one
  DEC- row: "Someone must check press-and-hold on a real iPhone".
- Do not break dragging, pinch-zoom, tap-to-select or double-tap-to-
  edit on touch screens.
- Do not change which menu entries a person may see (the capability
  checks in `handleContextMenu` stay).
- The menu must not open twice on Android, where the browser already
  sends `contextmenu`.
</executor_constraints>

<steps>
1. Reproduce on a real iPhone or iPad (Safari): open a canvas with a
   note, press and hold the note for about a second. Write down what
   happens (menu opens / nothing / text-selection bubble). Do the same
   on an Android phone if one is at hand.
2. If the menu already opens on iOS, skip steps 3 and 4 and say so in
   the hand-off.
3. Add a hold handler for items in `CanvasItemLayer.tsx` (one place for
   all item types): on `touchstart` with one finger, start a 500 ms
   timer and remember the start position. Cancel the timer on
   `touchend`, `touchcancel`, a second finger, or when the finger moves
   more than 10px (that is a drag). When the timer fires, call the same
   function right-click calls, with the touch position as the menu
   position, and mark the item as selected.
4. Stop double opening: after the hold opens the menu, ignore a
   `contextmenu` event for the next 700 ms. Also stop the hold from
   starting a drag of the item.
5. `RemindersContent.tsx` empty text: replace
   `Right-click an item on a canvas and choose "Remind me…" to see it
   again on a day you pick.` with
   `Open an item's menu on a canvas (right-click, or press and hold on
   a phone) and choose "Remind me…" to see it again on a day you pick.`
6. Search for other "right-click" instructions shown to people
   (`grep -rni "right-click\|right click" src`) and give each the same
   "or press and hold on a phone" wording.
7. Tests: unit-test the hold logic with fake timers
   (`tests/unit/canvas-press-and-hold.test.ts`): 500 ms hold opens;
   moving 20px cancels; lifting at 300 ms cancels; two fingers cancel;
   a `contextmenu` right after a hold does not open a second menu.
   Extract the timing logic into a small pure helper so it can be
   tested without the canvas.
</steps>

<acceptance>
- On an iPhone, holding a note for half a second opens the item menu at
  the finger; "Remind me…" can be chosen from it.
- Dragging a note, pinch-zoom, tap and double-tap work as before.
- On Android the menu opens once.
- The Reminders page no longer gives a mouse-only instruction.
- The hand-off names the real devices used, or says none were.
</acceptance>
