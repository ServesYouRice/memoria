# IMP-072 - Moving an item to another board leaves no broken lines

<objective>
When the ask palette (IMP-068) or auto-filing (IMP-066) moves an item to another board, its connector lines do not stay behind on the old board pointing at an item that is no longer there.
</objective>

<files>
`src/lib/ai/ask-commands.ts` (move execution), `src/lib/agents/service-core.ts` (`CanvasItem:MOVE` revert), `ItemConnection` in `prisma/schema.prisma`
</files>

<acceptance>
- A move removes the item's `ItemConnection` rows on the old board (both directions) in the same transaction, and records them in the ChangeSet.
- Undo puts the item back and restores those connections.
- Collaborators on the old board stop seeing the lines (realtime event as for a normal delete).
</acceptance>

<verification>
`pnpm test -- --run` (move drops and undo restores connections); `pnpm gate integration` for the transaction.
</verification>
