# IMP-077 - Dark mode: activity box and notes follow the theme

<objective>
Nothing stays bright white or glaring yellow in dark mode. Today the
dashboard's "Recent Activity" box stays white after a full page load, and
canvas notes keep their bright daytime yellow.
</objective>

<context>
Found in the 2026-11-11 UI review on a local dev build. On a hard reload of
`/dashboard` in dark mode the panel with
`data-testid="activity-feed-panel"` (`DashboardContent.tsx`) computes to
`rgb(255, 255, 255)` while the rest of the page is dark; the likely cause is
the first paint using the light theme before the saved choice is applied
(`src/lib/theme-context.tsx`, `src/lib/theme-preference.ts`). Canvas notes
use fixed colours in `NoteItem.tsx` (`#FFFACD` fill, `#FFD700` border,
`#333` text) and the read-only layer repeats them.
</context>

<files>
`src/features/dashboard/components/DashboardContent.tsx`,
`src/lib/theme-context.tsx`, `src/lib/theme-preference.ts`,
`src/features/canvas/components/NoteItem.tsx`,
`src/features/canvas/components/ReadonlyCanvasItemLayer.tsx`,
`tests/unit/dashboard-activity-feed.test.tsx`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
Fix the cause of the white panel, not only this one panel: check other
panels and cards after a hard reload in dark mode. Light-mode notes stay as
they are in this card (IMP-085 redesigns them).
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- In dark mode, after a hard reload of `/dashboard`, the activity box and
  every other panel match the dark theme, with no flash of white boxes.
- In dark mode, notes use a softer fill with at least 4.5:1 text contrast,
  in the editor and in the read-only/shared view.
- A regression test covers the dark-mode panel.
</acceptance>

<verification>
`pnpm test:file tests/unit/dashboard-activity-feed.test.tsx`;
`pnpm gate fast`; `pnpm gate e2e` on the dev machine; screenshots of the
dashboard and a canvas in dark mode after a hard reload.
</verification>
