# IMP-082 - Sign-in and sign-up pages give the form the room

<objective>
Signing in feels calm and quick: the form is the main thing on the page, and
when someone is locked out the message says how long to wait.
</objective>

<context>
The 2026-11-11 UI review found that on desktop the purple side panel in
`AuthLayout.tsx` takes about 63% of the width and repeats marketing text,
squeezing the form. `LoginForm.tsx` shows "Too many sign-in attempts. Please
wait and try again." with no hint of how long.
</context>

<files>
`src/features/auth/components/AuthLayout.tsx`,
`src/features/auth/components/LoginForm.tsx`,
`src/features/auth/components/RegisterForm.tsx`,
`tests/unit/login-form-errors.test.tsx`,
`tests/e2e/visual/auth-visual.spec.ts`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
No change to how sign-in, passkeys, two-step codes or rate limits work on
the server. `RegisterForm.tsx` keeps stating the launch limits (launch
contract test).
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- On desktop the form column is at least half the width, with fields at
  least 44px tall; the side panel shows a canvas picture and one short line.
  On a phone the panel shrinks to the logo.
- Passkey sign-in is as easy to spot as the password button where enabled.
- The lock-out message says how long to wait: the server's retry time when
  the response carries one, otherwise the length of the limit window.
- Errors stay announced to screen readers and tied to their fields.
</acceptance>

<verification>
`pnpm test:file tests/unit/login-form-errors.test.tsx`; `pnpm gate fast`;
`pnpm gate e2e` on the dev machine with the auth visual baselines refreshed;
screenshots of sign-in and sign-up at 1440x900 and 390x844, light and dark.
</verification>
