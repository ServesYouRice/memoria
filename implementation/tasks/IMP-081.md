# IMP-081 - Home page shows the product

<objective>
A first-time visitor sees what Memoria is within a few seconds: a picture of
a real canvas, plain words and one obvious button.
</objective>

<context>
The 2026-11-11 UI review found that `src/app/page.tsx` describes the product
only in words, some of them insider terms ("explicit save feedback", a
"Launch limits: ..." line in the hero, "Everything you need"), never shows a
canvas, and gives two buttons equal weight. The launch contract test
(`tests/unit/launch-surface-contract.test.ts`) requires the page to state
the enforced limits and the export formats and bans unearned claims; that
honesty rule stays.
</context>

<files>
`src/app/page.tsx`, `public/`, `tests/unit/launch-surface-contract.test.ts`,
`tests/e2e/launch-surface.spec.ts`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
- Keep the registration-mode behaviour (open, invite, closed) as it is.
- The limits stay on the page, in plain words near the sign-up button, not
  in the hero. Do not weaken the launch contract test.
- No new third-party resources. The hero picture has alt text, fixed
  dimensions (no layout jump) and works in light and dark.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- The hero shows a real canvas with notes, a link card, a picture and a
  drawing, next to a plain-words headline and one main button; signing in is
  a quiet link.
- Three short "what you can do" blocks, each with a small picture; no
  insider wording anywhere on the page.
- The bottom sign-up button is readable (IMP-075).
- No sideways scroll at 390px wide; the page passes the axe checks.
</acceptance>

<verification>
`pnpm test:file tests/unit/launch-surface-contract.test.ts`;
`pnpm gate fast`; `pnpm gate build`; `pnpm gate e2e` on the dev machine;
screenshots at 1440x900 and 390x844, light and dark.
</verification>
