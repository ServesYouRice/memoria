# IMP-101 - Ask about one canvas or one tag

<objective>
A question can be limited to one canvas or one tag, so answers come from
the right pile instead of everything the person has saved.
</objective>

<context>
Approved by the owner on 2026-11-11. "Ask" always searches all of the
person's canvases: `askRequestSchema` (`src/lib/ai/ask-schema.ts`) accepts
only `question`, and the palette (`AskPalette.tsx`) has no way to narrow
it. AI on shared canvases is disabled for launch.
</context>

<files>
`src/lib/ai/ask-schema.ts`, `src/lib/ai/ask.ts`,
`src/app/api/v1/ai/ask/route.ts`,
`src/features/command-palette/AskPalette.tsx`,
`tests/unit/ai-ask.test.ts`, `tests/api/ai-ask-route.test.ts`.
</files>

<executor_constraints>
The scope is validated on the server: the canvas must be the person's own,
and a scope never widens what the question may read. The rule that the
model sees labels, not real ids, stays.
</executor_constraints>

<acceptance>
- The Ask box shows where it is looking ("All canvases") and lets the
  person switch to one canvas or one tag.
- Opened inside a canvas, it offers "This canvas" first.
- Answers and their sources come only from the chosen scope, and the
  answer says which scope was used.
- A canvas the person does not own is refused.
</acceptance>
