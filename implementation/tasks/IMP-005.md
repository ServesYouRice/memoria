# IMP-005 — Lockout without victim denial-of-service

<objective>
Brute-force attempts slow down without letting five guesses against a known
email repeatedly lock the victim out.
</objective>

<context>
Start after IMP-004 so trusted client identity is available.
</context>

<files>
`src/lib/auth/account-lockout.ts`, `src/lib/auth-options.ts`,
`src/middleware/auth-rate-limit.ts`, `src/lib/cache/redis-client.ts`.
</files>

<steps>
1. Replace email-only hard lockout with per-principal/client escalation plus a
   bounded account-wide delay. Keep unknown and known accounts indistinguishable.
2. Define one production Redis-failure behavior and test it; do not silently
   reset attempts to one.
3. Allow a correct credential to recover safely according to the chosen policy.
4. Add owner notification/unlock only if email delivery is durable; otherwise
   keep copy honest and operator recovery documented.
5. Test concurrent attempts, Redis failure, expiry, correct-password recovery,
   and unknown accounts.
</steps>

<acceptance>
- One attacker/client cannot continuously lock a victim out.
- Brute-force cost still rises and timing does not enumerate accounts.
</acceptance>

<verification>
Targeted auth/Redis tests; `pnpm type-check`; `pnpm lint`.
</verification>
