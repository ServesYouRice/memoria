# IMP-022 — Accessible and responsive canvas

<objective>
Meet DEC-009 with a real DOM representation of canvas content and usable
keyboard, screen-reader, mobile, and theme behavior.
</objective>

<files>
`src/features/canvas/components/CanvasBoard.tsx`,
`src/features/canvas/components/CanvasItemLayer.tsx`,
`src/features/canvas/components/ReadonlyCanvasItemLayer.tsx`,
`src/features/canvas/components/MainToolbar.tsx`, `src/app/share/[token]/`,
`src/lib/theme-context.tsx`, `tests/`, `percy.yml`.
</files>

<steps>
1. Add a DOM item list for owned, shared, and public canvases with type, heading,
   content summary, tags, selection, and permitted actions.
2. Define Tab traversal, Enter edit, arrow nudge, create/delete/recover, focus,
   announcements, and non-spatial connection semantics. Keep capability roles.
3. Redesign dense canvas/public controls for 320/375/768/1024 widths with a
   prioritized toolbar and overflow/bottom sheet.
4. Drive editor/canvas colors from the selected app theme; honor reduced motion.
5. Add axe and keyboard automation; perform the selected NVDA/VoiceOver manual
   protocol. Include public share and both themes.
</steps>

<acceptance>
- Core content is discoverable and operable without pointer/canvas pixels.
- Automated checks are mandatory, not environment-skipped release evidence.
</acceptance>

<verification>
Playwright axe/keyboard/responsive tests plus the recorded manual protocol;
type/lint.
</verification>

<status>
Steps 1 to 4 are implemented and verified. Step 5 is partially done.

Delivered:
- `CanvasItemListView` and `CanvasAccessiblePanel` render canvas content as
  real DOM for owned, shared, and public canvases. The panel is always in the
  accessibility tree — clipped, never `display: none` or `hidden`.
- Tab traversal, Enter to edit, arrow-key nudge (10px with Shift), Delete with
  a recovery pointer, focus-driven selection, and a polite live region.
  Connections are described by the items they link, not by coordinates.
- `CanvasSecondaryActions` collapses the dense header into one overflow menu
  below `md`; every action keeps its accessible name in both layouts.
- Canvas skeleton colors come from the theme; reduced motion is honored.
- 32 unit tests cover the keyboard contract, capability gating per role, and
  the 320/375/768/1024 layouts.

Outstanding — do not treat this card as fully closed:
1. No Playwright axe automation. `@axe-core/playwright` is not a dependency,
   and the acceptance criterion requires automated checks to be mandatory
   release evidence rather than environment-skipped. Adding it needs a
   Playwright run against a live app, which the unit suite cannot replace.
2. The NVDA/VoiceOver manual protocol has not been performed or recorded.
   It needs a human on real assistive technology; both themes and the public
   share route are in scope.
3. `percy.yml` visual coverage for the new panel and the collapsed toolbar has
   not been added.

Until 1 and 2 are done, the DEC-009 launch bar ("accessible DOM item list
before public launch") is met in implementation but not in recorded evidence.
</status>
