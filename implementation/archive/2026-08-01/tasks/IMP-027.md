# IMP-027 — Share invitation and notification lifecycle

<objective>
Email sharing has explicit invitation consent and meaningful recipient activity,
using durable delivery.
</objective>

<files>
`src/app/api/v1/canvases/[canvasId]/share/route.ts`,
`src/app/api/v1/canvases/[canvasId]/share/[shareId]/route.ts`,
`src/features/canvas/components/ShareDialog.tsx`, `src/lib/email/templates.ts`,
`src/lib/activity.ts`, `src/app/notifications/`, `prisma/schema.prisma`.
</files>

<steps>
1. Add expiring single-use accept/decline invitation state tied to normalized
   email and canvas/role. Prevent enumeration and cap pending shares.
2. Migrate accepted shares to stable user identity; define verified email-change
   behavior and revoke stale pending invites.
3. Deliver invite and decision email through IMP-014/015. Preserve safe
   same-origin return-to from IMP-002.
4. Separate actor, subject, recipients, read state, and preferences. Rename the
   existing page to “Your activity” until recipient notifications ship.
5. Test accept/decline/expiry/replay, email change, role revocation, owner
   visibility, and delivery retry.
</steps>

<acceptance>
- A share never silently transfers to a future owner of an old email address.
- “Notifications” contains recipient events or is labeled as actor activity.
</acceptance>

<verification>
Real-DB invitation/activity tests; production email E2E; type/lint.
</verification>
