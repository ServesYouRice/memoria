<goal>
Make ShareDialog (src/features/canvas/components/ShareDialog.tsx) discard checkPublicStatus results that were requested before a public-link mutation or before the dialog closed, and keep the public controls disabled until the initial status settles.

This matters because it fixes:
- An older status response can make a public canvas appear private (medium)
  The initial status request is neither cancelled nor checked for staleness, and controls remain enabled during that request. A GET that captured the private state can finish after a successful enable operation and overwrite isPublic with false. The switch then appears off and hides the link while public access remains enabled on the server.
</goal>
