<goal>
Route every broadcast send in src/lib/collaboration/websocket-server.ts through one helper that checks ws.bufferedAmount. The helper skips ephemeral fanout above a soft limit and closes consumers above a hard limit. The heartbeat also closes a client that stays over the hard limit even while it answers pings.

This matters because it fixes:
- Broadcasts do not bound slow clients' outbound queues (medium)
  Broadcast helpers send whenever a socket is OPEN without checking queued outbound bytes or applying a slow-consumer limit. ws can continue buffering messages when the recipient cannot drain them. Incoming rate limits do not bound this accumulated fanout, and unsolicited pong frames can keep a deliberately slow recipient marked alive.
</goal>
