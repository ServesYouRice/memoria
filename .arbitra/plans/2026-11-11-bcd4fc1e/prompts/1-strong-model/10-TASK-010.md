- Home-board accept and auto-file are not atomic: a crash, a transient error or a concurrent dismiss leaves an executed move marked DISMISSED with no changeSetId, so it cannot be undone (medium)
  The model keeps one HomeBoardSuggestion row per item, forever. Nothing marks the row as in progress before the move runs. This causes three problems. (1) If the process dies or the DB call fails after the move but before the last updateMany, the row stays OPEN with no changeSetId.
