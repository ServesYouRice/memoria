<goal>
Make revertChangeSet (src/lib/agents/service-core.ts) safe against concurrent duplicate reverts and against lock-order deadlocks for user-scoped, multi-canvas change sets.

This matters because it fixes:
- Concurrent rollbacks bypass the already-reverted guard (medium)
  The change set and revertedAt guard are read before entering the transaction. Two requests can both pass, then serialize on the canvas lock without rechecking the change set. Both subsequently apply its inverse and create completed rollback histories.
- Multi-canvas archive undo violates the archive lock order (medium)
  <quotes> await lockCanvasForMutation(tx, existing.canvasId); </quotes> Archiving acquires all canvas locks in sorted order, but archive undo acquires them lazily in change-record order. Archive change sets have user scope, so revertChangeSet does not pre-lock their canvases. An undo that locks B before A can deadlock against an archive that locks A before B.
</goal>
